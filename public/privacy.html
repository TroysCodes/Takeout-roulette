Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short version

Takeout Roulette is a small, local app that helps you pick takeout in Hamilton. We only collect what we need to run it and to send you one email a week. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you unlock the Spin button or create an account.
  • Your password, if you create an account. It is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your lists, visits and ratings, if you have an account, so they follow you to any device. Without an account they stay only on your device.
  • Restaurant ratings and comments you choose to share. These are shown publicly on restaurant cards without your name or email.
  • Visit counts. Your browser gets a random ID so we can count visitors. It isn't linked to your name. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies.
  • One sign-in cookie, only if you sign in, so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your spins, lists and account.
  • To send you at most one email a week with takeout picks and Takeout Roulette news, because you agreed to it.
  • To see which restaurants people love, so we can improve the list.

The weekly email and unsubscribing

You can stop the weekly email any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply to it and ask. We'll stop within 10 business days. Unsubscribing doesn't delete your account.

Who can see your information

Only the founder of Takeout Roulette can see the email list and account details. We don't sell, rent or trade your information. It is stored with the companies that run our website and database (Vercel and Upstash), which may keep it on servers outside Canada.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you use Takeout Roulette. You can ask us to show you what we have, fix it, or delete it. Reply to any of our emails and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is big, we'll tell you by email.
